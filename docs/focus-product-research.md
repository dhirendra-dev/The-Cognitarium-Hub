# Focus Product Research Notes

The Cognitarium Focus Lab is an original educational experience. Its product scope was informed by publicly described focus patterns from Pomodoro and digital-wellbeing tools; it does not copy any third-party branding or interface.

| Public reference | Relevant public feature patterns adapted for students | Cognitarium treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Regain product site](https://regainapp.co/) | Custom focus sessions, break timing, habit insights, and intentional interruption boundaries. | Configurable focus, short-break, and long-break cycles; private distraction reflection; weekly focus insight cards. |
| [Regain on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.regainapp&hl=en_US) | Pomodoro, stopwatch, custom durations, focus preparation, accountability, and scheduling concepts. | Pomodoro, custom-duration, and stopwatch modes; readiness checklist; subject and intention setup; dashboard access. Device-level app blocking and background enforcement are not claimed because a web app cannot securely provide them. |
| [Regain on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/regain-screen-time-control/id6749575953) | Screen-time awareness, focus sessions, app limits, and routine building. | Focus-session logging, weekly attention insight, reset routines, and transparent device-boundary guidance. |

## Product boundary

The website can help a student plan, run, and reflect on a study block. It cannot inspect, block, or limit apps and websites on a student’s device. Any device-level focus mode remains under the student’s control.
